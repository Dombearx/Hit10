# Google Play listing (docs/07 M6)

Limits: name ≤ 30 characters, short description ≤ 80, full ≤ 4000
(`tools/store/check_listing.py` counts them). Graphics, regenerated with
`make store`: `icon-512.png`, `feature-pl.png` / `feature-en.png`
(1024×500, one per language) and `screens/pl/1–7.png`,
`screens/en/1–7.png` (1080×1920, 9:16).

Choices behind the texts (Play metadata policy and store-listing best
practice):

- The brand **Hit5** stays the same in every language; the part after the
  colon is translated and carries the words people search for
  (stoper / stopwatch, 5.00). No emoji, caps or claims ("best", "#1",
  "free") in the name or short description.
- The short description says what you do and why it keeps you playing,
  in one sentence each.
- The first lines of the full description are what Play shows before
  "More": the hook and the genre. Numbers match the game (14 clocks,
  11 skills, 149 achievements, 18 perks, 7 seasonal events).
- No mention of other games, no prices, no requests for ratings.

## English (en-US)

**Name:** Hit5: Stop the Watch at 5.00

**Short description:** Stop the watch at exactly 5.00. Collect clocks that earn Ticks for you.

**Full description:**

Stop the watch at exactly 5.00. Sounds easy? Go on, try.

Hit5 is a timing game meets idle clicker. Every round lasts five
seconds. The closer you get to 5.00, the more Ticks you earn: PERFECT,
GREAT, GOOD… or TOO EARLY. Spend Ticks on clocks that try to hit 5.00
too — and keep earning while you're away.

⏱️ 14 clocks
From a grandpa with a pocket watch, a grandma with an egg timer and a
cuckoo, through a watchmaker and a sprint judge, all the way to an
atomic clock, a pulsar, a time machine, Schrödinger's cat and Chronos
himself. Over a hundred upgrades, each with its own sense of humour.

🎯 11 skills
Strike Power, Late Start, Cold Blood, Hawk Eye, Combo, Shield, Luck,
Golden Touch and more — each one changes how you play a round.

✨ The Golden Second
Catch the flying golden clock for a Frenzy (everything ×7), a Lucky
payout or a Hot Hand.

🌀 Time paradox
Rewind time, earn Chronons for a permanent bonus and unlock 18 perks —
from a head start to a second clock.

🏆 Plenty to do
149 achievements, a daily bonus, Blind mode and 7 seasonal events
through the year.

📴 Offline, no account
Plays without internet and your progress stays on your phone. Ads are
rewarded only, and you watch one only when you pick a bonus.
